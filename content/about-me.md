## Why this blog exists

It was a difficult time. The kind where you lose yourself so gradually 
you don't notice until you're already gone. Somewhere along the way I 
lost the part of me that learned just for the joy of it.

What brought me back was a journey mapped in ink — from **Euphoria** 
to **Serendipity** to heartbreak, through the **Magic Shop** where fear 
becomes comfort, past the pain of **Tear**, all the way to the 
**Love Yourself** heart and finally **Epiphany** — the realization that 
i should have been Loving myself who i am as a person before even 
venturing out into the world.

*I'm Fine* — I carry those words as a reminder to myself. Read them 
backwards (On my tattoo read it backwards not here 🙃) and they say something else entirely. Anyone who's been through 
something difficult will understand.

This blog — **ತತ್ವ – 見性 – 여정** — is part of that same journey. 
Essence. Awakening. Journey. Three words from three civilizations that 
mean one thing: the curious mind walks toward the light.

Every month, one thing. Written honestly. For anyone who ever forgot 
why they loved learning — or why they loved being alive.

> *"I'm the one I should love in this world."* — Jin, Epiphany
