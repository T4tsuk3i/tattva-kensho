Hey, I'm **Tatsuya** — also known as T4tsuk3i on the internet.

From Mangalore, living in Bangalore, six months away from becoming a lawyer. But if you came here expecting someone who just talks about law — wrong place, right person.

I'm a cybersecurity researcher, an AI tinkerer, a generative artist, a gamer, a cyclist, and now apparently a blogger. I don't do one thing. I do everything that pulls at my curiosity — and then I pull it apart to see how it works.

I've been building an AI assistant that's as sharp as it is snarky. I've been digging into OSINT, reverse engineering, LLM internals, and automation. I run Arch Linux — CachyOS specifically — because embracing complexity with elegance is kind of my whole thing.

> *"Everything can be reverse engineered — even expectations."*

## Why this blog exists

Somewhere between deadlines, backlogs, and the pressure of becoming something, I lost the part of me that learned just for the joy of it.

This blog is me finding that again.

**ತತ್ವ – 見性 – 여정** — Essence. Awakening. Journey. Three words from three civilizations that together mean one thing: the curious mind walks toward the light.

Every month, one thing. Simple or complex, scientific or philosophical, ancient or new. Written so that anyone — young or old — can pick it up and feel that spark again.
