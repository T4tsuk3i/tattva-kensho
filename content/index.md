*Essence. Awakening. Journey.*

A person rediscovering his roots — from Mangalore, now navigating 
life in Bangalore. Somewhere between deadlines and the pressure of 
becoming something, I lost the part of me that learned just for 
the joy of it. This blog is my reset.

Not to teach, not to lecture — but to help people rediscover why 
we learn at all. Because learning will always be a story that never 
ends. And the longer it goes, the more interesting it gets.

A researcher, tinkerer, coder, gamer, artist — who believes that 
the joy of learning never really leaves you. It just gets buried.

Every month, one thing. Simple or complex, scientific or 
philosophical, ancient or new. Written so that anyone — young 
or old — can pick it up and feel that spark again.

This is my new beginning. Welcome to the journey.
